Kaitiakitanga and Manaakitanga

Green Future promotes kaitiakitanga by encouraging people to care for the environment through reducing waste, recycling, saving water and protecting nature. It also promotes manaakitanga by encouraging people to work together and care for others by creating a cleaner, healthier environment for future generations. Small actions by everyone can make a postitive difference to our communities and our planet.
